# Raw notes: recognizability anchors (verified by search, not assumed)

Reinterpreted criterion per Jeroen's steer: the domain must be recognizable to ordinary people; the docs reader is still a developer, but the fictional form user is not.

## Event run-of-show

- "Run of show" / "run sheet" / "day-of timeline" is an established, named planning artifact with abundant templates and guides:
  - https://www.nunify.com/blogs/wedding-run-of-show (template + timeline guide)
  - https://www.minted.com/wedding-ideas/wedding-reception-timeline
  - https://blissandbone.com/resources/wedding-day-timeline
  - https://thestagemc.com.au/blog/wedding-reception-run-sheet-template (an MC's working run sheet)
  - https://invitecount.com/guides/wedding-day-timeline-template (free spreadsheet template)
- Conventional order verified: grand entrance, first dance, welcome toast, dinner, speeches, parent dances, cake cutting, open dancing, send-off; 4 to 5 hour envelope; speech blocks of 20 to 25 minutes slotted around courses.
- Curfew reality verified:
  - https://www.felixandfingers.com/blog/wedding-resources/wedding-venue-sound-limits-what-to-confirm-before-finalizing-your-entertainment/
  - https://www.insideweddings.com/news/planning-design/music-restrictions-you-might-not-expect/41621
  - https://www.ce-eventproductions.com/post/understanding-noise-ordinances-curfews-and-permits-for-your-westchester-ny-wedding
  - Common pattern: amplified music legally or contractually ends 21:00 to 23:00 (ordinance, license/planning condition, or neighbour agreement); restrictions bind amplified sound, not the event itself.
- Everyone has attended a wedding or planned a party; no technical knowledge needed to understand why the band must finish before the curfew.

## Workout plan builder

- Ordering rules are published coaching orthodoxy, not invented for the demo:
  - https://www.aworkoutroutine.com/exercise-order/ (big before small, compound before isolation)
  - https://hypertrophy.towerofrecords.com/hypertrophy/exercise-order (compound-first principle)
  - https://www.spcfitz.com/how-to-structure-a-workout (warm-up, compounds, isolation, core, cool-down)
  - Pre-exhaustion as the known exception: https://t-nation.com/t/isolations-or-compounds-first/281024
- Gym-goer recognition is broad (workout-builder apps are mainstream), though a notch below "everyone has been to a wedding."
