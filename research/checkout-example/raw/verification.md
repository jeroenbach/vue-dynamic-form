## Corrections log

No claims refuted. Two usage rules for the docs page:
1. Never name Best Buy as the $300M retailer; say "a major retailer" as Spool does.
2. Attribute the 15%/36%/50% numbers explicitly to "a study Shopify commissioned from one of the Big Three consulting firms", not as independent fact.
